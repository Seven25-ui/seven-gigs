Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Seven Gigs ("we", "us", "the site") collects when you visit, and how it is used. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask visitors to create an account or submit personal details to read the site. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services we use may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.

If you email us through the contact page, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

Store purchases

If you buy a digital product from our store, you pay through GCash or PayPal and then email us your receipt or payment reference and the email address where we should send your file. We may also see your name and mobile number as shown in a GCash transaction, or the details shown in a PayPal transaction. We use this information only to confirm your payment, deliver your file, and answer questions about your order. GCash and PayPal process the payment themselves. We do not see or store your login details, PIN, or card information.

AI chat assistant

Some pages have an AI chat assistant called Seven AI. When you send a message, the text you type, along with the recent messages of that chat, is sent through our server on Cloudflare to Google's Gemini service so it can write a reply. Please do not share personal, financial, or sensitive information in the chat.

Your chat is kept in your browser's session storage so it stays visible while you browse, and it is cleared when you close the tab or clear your site data. To limit abuse, our server temporarily keeps a count of messages linked to your IP address for up to one hour. Cloudflare and Google may also process technical data such as your IP address, as described in their own privacy policies. AI replies can be wrong, so please do not treat them as professional, legal, tax, or financial advice.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site.
  • To understand which content is useful to readers.
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To answer your questions through the AI chat assistant.
  • To confirm payments and deliver purchased files.
  • To show advertising that helps fund the site.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. The site and third parties we work with may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, and serve ads. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some features work.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type, and browser. We use this information in aggregate form and do not use it to identify you personally. You can opt out by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

Reading suggestions

To suggest posts you may like, the site saves a short list of the posts you have read in your browser's local storage. This list stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners, including third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be shown a consent message so they can choose how their data is used for advertising.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Supabase to store and serve blog posts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, traffic measurement is provided by Google Analytics, and the AI chat assistant runs on Cloudflare and Google Gemini. These providers may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests content from them. Please review their privacy policies for details.

Links to other sites

Posts may link to third-party websites and tools. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
