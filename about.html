About Seven Gigs

Freelancing and AI, in plain language.

Seven Gigs is a blog for freelancers who want to work faster and win more clients using AI tools.

Who writes this

I'm Gene, a freelancer on Upwork who builds websites, chatbots, and automations. I write about the things I actually use and test: how to write proposals that sound human, which AI tools are worth your time, and how to build small projects step by step.

What you'll find here

  • Freelancing: finding clients, writing proposals, and avoiding common mistakes.
  • AI Tools: honest looks at AI tools, including free options for people just starting out.
  • Tutorials: step-by-step guides for small projects such as bots, websites, and automations.

How I write

Every post is written by me from my own experience and testing. I aim to keep things practical, up to date, and honest about what works and what doesn't. If a tool or method changes, I update the post.
