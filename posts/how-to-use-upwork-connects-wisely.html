How to Use Upwork Connects Wisely

Nov 1, 2026 · 2 min read

When you are new on Upwork, Connects feel like free tickets. They are not. Each proposal uses several of them, and once they are gone, you need to buy more or wait. I learned to treat them like a small budget, and my applying got a lot smarter.

1. Set a daily limit

I send one proposal per day. It sounds slow, but it forces me to pick the best job instead of spraying proposals everywhere. One good, personal proposal beats five rushed ones.

2. Apply early

Jobs with few proposals give you a real chance of being seen. Once a job has 50 or more proposals, I usually skip it. I check new jobs in the morning and apply to the ones that were just posted.

3. Skip jobs you cannot do

If a job asks for a tool or skill you have never used, do not apply and hope for the best. I skip those. A rejected proposal wastes Connects and does not teach you anything.

4. Check the client first

Before spending Connects, look at the client's payment verification, rating, and spend history. A job from a client with no history is not always bad, but it is a bigger risk. If you want to know the warning signs, read my post on client red flags.

5. Read the Connects cost

Different jobs ask for different amounts. A small job that costs a lot of Connects needs to be worth it. I compare the cost to the pay before I decide.

6. Think before you boost

Boosting costs extra Connects. When your Connects are limited, I think it is better to save them for more proposals than to boost one. This is my choice as a beginner, and it may change later.

7. Pick jobs that match your portfolio

I attach portfolio projects that are close to the job. A support chatbot job gets my chatbot project. An automation job gets my n8n workflow. Clients can see the match right away, and your Connects work harder.

8. Look for free Connects

Upwork sometimes gives free Connects for completing parts of your profile. When I added a video introduction, I was able to get some. Check your profile page for current offers, since they can change.

9. Keep a simple log

I write down the job, the date, the Connects used, and the price I offered. After a few weeks, you can see what kind of jobs get replies and what kind do not.

Your Connects will not last forever, but good habits make them last longer. Choose fewer jobs, apply earlier, and write every proposal like a real person wrote it.
